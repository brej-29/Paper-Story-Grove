A note for grown-ups

Let your child pick a book and turn the pages. When a picture catches their eye, try asking “What do you think will happen if we touch it?” The star counter offers a hint. At the end, Try the other way takes you back to the choice.

All six stories come in Hand-drawn. Choose Higgsfield on the shelf to read Anu, Dev or Moss with moving paintings. The words and endings are the same in both editions.

For a quieter read, switch off Sound and turn on Calm motion. Taps still show captions. If you’d like someone to read along, Read to me uses an English voice when one is available on your device.

Inside the six books

These are original stories. Ammu, Meenu, Anu and Dev live in imagined households in Kerala and Tamil Nadu. Their stories draw on real places, languages and customs.
