A note for grown-ups

All six stories have a hand-drawn edition. Anu, Dev and Moss also have a Higgsfield edition with moving paintings. Here’s something to look for together in each book.

Anu and the Last Little Lamp

During Karthigai Deepam in Tamil Nadu, families light small clay lamps. Paatti, Anu’s grandmother, lights them while Anu looks out for their neighbour. Ask your child, “What could we do to let someone know we’re waiting for them?”

Dev and the Extra Banana Leaf

At Dev’s family feast for Onam in Kerala, each person eats from a banana leaf. His cousin Leela arrives, and the row is full. Before choosing an ending, lay out pretend leaves on your lap. Where could one more person sit?

Moss and the Kite That Sneezed

One enormous dragon sneeze tears Mouse’s kite. Pause when Moss admits what happened and let your child suggest a repair. After Mouse and Moss finish theirs, trace the new kite in the air together.

Ammu and the Monsoon Boat

Ammu and her grandfather follow a little paper boat through the Kerala backwaters. Listen for the rain together. What might Ammu hear if she follows the drums instead of the quiet lily channel?

Meenu and the Morning Kolam

A kolam is a doorstep pattern traditionally drawn with rice flour in Tamil Nadu. Tap the dots with Meenu, then let your child draw a loop on your palm. Will they put a peacock or a sun in the middle?

Puff, the Sleepy Little Cloud

Puff is still awake when the world is settling down. Copy a sleepy sound together, then let your child tuck Puff in. Would a cloud feel cosier beside the moon or curled up on a hill?
